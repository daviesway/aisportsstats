The Opportunity
Why Canada. Why Now.
01

Beyond Toronto

The NFL's Canadian strategy begins and ends with Toronto. Millions of passionate football fans from Vancouver to Halifax have no NFL team identity — until now.

02

The Stadium Solution

Flag football doesn't need a billion-dollar stadium. Canada's existing infrastructure is perfectly sized. The barrier to entry disappears entirely.

03

Olympic Pipeline

Flag football debuts at LA 2028. Canada has elite athletes ready — they just need an organized development pathway. The CFFL is that pathway, starting now.

04

Health Research

Canada's publicly funded health institutions are world-class. A CFFL partnership creates a research sandbox for concussion reduction that benefits the entire sport globally.

The Saskatchewan Proof of Concept

Saskatchewan has no NHL team. The Roughriders are not just a football team — they are the culture. Fans drive hours across the prairies to watch games. Under the CFFL model, Regina and Saskatoon each host a flag football team, creating a local rivalry that feeds directly into NFL engagement. If the CFFL works anywhere, it works here first. And if it works here, it works everywhere.
